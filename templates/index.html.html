Citizen-centric problem resolution

Report. Prioritize. Collaborate. Resolve.

JanSamadhan AI brings citizen reports, duplicate detection, safety alerts and institutional collaboration into one transparent workflow. Repeated reports are grouped, urgent cases are prioritized, and approved problem-solvers can work together on documented solutions.

01 · ReportCitizen submits title, category, location, description and optional photo.
02 · AI PrioritizationDuplicate matching and danger keywords help surface repeated or critical issues.
03 · CollaborationStudents, universities and industry partners contribute through approved accounts.
04 · ResolutionSolutions are recorded, progress is updated, and solved cases remain auditable.
