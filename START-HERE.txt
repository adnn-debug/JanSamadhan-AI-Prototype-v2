IMPORTANT TRUTH FOR THE JURY
The AI in this college prototype is an explainable rule-based simulation. Say that clearly.
It demonstrates the product workflow and the inputs/outputs expected from a future trained model.
Human validation remains required for priority, assignment and completion.

WHAT A PRODUCTION VERSION STILL NEEDS
- Server-side authentication, role authorization and secure administrator controls
- OTP or verified institutional identity instead of demo credentials
- A trained multilingual classification and semantic duplicate model
- GIS maps, geotag verification and jurisdiction routing
- SMS, WhatsApp, email and push notifications
- Secure object storage for evidence instead of data URLs in Firestore
- Formal consent, retention, audit log and content-moderation policies
- Government department APIs and SLA/escalation integrations
- Offline-first mobile/PWA reporting and assisted access kiosks
- Model monitoring, bias testing, appeal/correction workflow and security testing
