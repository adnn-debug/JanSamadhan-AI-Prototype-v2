IMPORTANT TRUTH FOR THE JURY
The prototype uses LLM-assisted decision support when Groq is configured and deterministic local fallback when it is unavailable.
The core automatic duplicate merge is still based on explainable text/concept, locality and category similarity. GIS adds separate nearby-case and coordinate-based recurrence signals where citizens provide a map pin.
AI and GIS signals are advisory. Government / Nodal Administrator remains the case owner and human validation controls routing and completion.
Do not claim the current prototype is a trained proprietary model, vector-search system or production-ready government platform.

WHAT A PRODUCTION VERSION STILL NEEDS
- Server-side authentication, verified role authorization and secure administrator controls
- OTP or verified institutional identity instead of demo credentials
- Vector/embedding semantic search and trained multilingual classification where justified
- Verified geocoding, jurisdiction boundaries and department routing rules
- SMS, WhatsApp, email and push notifications
- Secure object storage for evidence instead of data URLs in Firestore
- Formal consent, retention, audit log and content-moderation policies
- Government department APIs and official SLA/escalation integrations
- Offline-first mobile/PWA reporting and assisted access kiosks
- Model monitoring, bias testing, appeal/correction workflow and security testing
